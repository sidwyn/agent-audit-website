## Email automation

Manual emails don't scale past 20. This stack runs the whole campaign in 2–3 hours of setup plus 15 minutes a day handling replies.

### Step 0 — verify and expand
Store Leads (storeleads.app). Upload the 100 domains, confirm platform = Shopify and revenue band, then use the same filters (Shopify, $1M–$50M est., US, your verticals) to pull the next 900. To sell 20 audits you'll want 500–1,000 prospects total; this list is the seed, not the campaign.

### Step 1 — find contacts
Apollo.io (free tier covers the first few hundred lookups), verified with Findymail or Hunter. Titles in priority order: founder, CEO, head of ecommerce. At the bigger stores, also pull the finance or ops lead — that's who feels the dispute exposure.

### Step 2 — personalize with the product itself
This is the edge. Don't write personalized emails; generate personalized findings. A script takes each domain and runs the audit's automated half — robots.txt agent rules, schema.org Product/Offer markup, feed presence, checkout reachability — and emits one line per store:

> "Ran a quick check on [store]: your product pages are missing offer-level markup, so ChatGPT can't read your prices."

That line becomes a merge field in email 1. Nobody ignores a real finding about their own store, and the script is also the automated half of the audit deliverable — building it is product work, not marketing overhead. Roughly a day to build; say the word and I'll write it.

No-code alternative: Clay (clay.com, $149+/mo) does waterfall enrichment plus AI-written first lines. Skip it — generic AI personalization reads as spam, and the script's findings are real.

### Step 3 — sending infrastructure
Instantly.ai or Smartlead ($37–97/mo). Rules that keep you out of spam:
- Never send cold from your main domain. Buy two lookalikes (e.g. getagentproof.com, agentproof.co)
- 2–3 Google Workspace inboxes per domain, warmed in-tool for 2–3 weeks before the first real send
- 20–25 cold emails per inbox per day. Six inboxes ≈ 120–150/day, so volume is never the constraint — list size is
- Zero links in email 1. Links tank deliverability and you want a reply, not a click

### Step 4 — sequence
Already written in [[agent-audit-marketing]]:
1. Day 1 — warm DM founder variant + the generated finding from step 2
2. Day 4 — the finding post data ("ran [agent] against [N] stores, [X] failed at [stage]")
3. Day 8 — the existing follow-up ("do you know what percent of last month's orders came through an assistant?")

Stop after three. No "just bumping this."

### Step 5 — replies and booking
Replies land in your real inbox; everything after the first reply is manual and should be. Send the Cal.com link only once they've replied.

### Compliance and account safety
- CAN-SPAM: physical mailing address in the footer, working opt-out ("reply 'no' and I won't email again" qualifies), honor it same-day
- Never automate X or LinkedIn DMs — the tools that do this get accounts banned. The ~15 founders marked "DM" above are a manual job: 10 a day, personalized, done in under half an hour

### Expected math
Verified founder inboxes plus a real per-store finding: 40–60% open, 5–10% reply. The 100 above → 5–10 conversations → 2–4 audits sold. Hitting 20 audits means working the full 500–1,000 list from step 0, which this same stack handles without added effort.
