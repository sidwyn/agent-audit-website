## Two things are true about agent traffic
**Agents convert.** AI shopping traffic is growing fast and converts better than the rest. You want agents to be
able to buy from you.

**Agents probe.** Same channel — no checkout screen, no friction. An agent can test every promo code, read every
price, and pull your exact stock, and it arrives looking like ordinary traffic. That's the side AgentArmor watches.

## What it stops
**Price & stock scraping.** Competitors pull your catalog — prices, SKUs, exact stock — to undercut you
automatically. AgentArmor hides the sensitive fields from untrusted agents and rate-limits the scrapers. *(In one
audit, a store leaked the exact stock on its near-sellout items — the very numbers a competitor wants most.)*

**Promo-code farming.** Bot farms drain new-customer and single-use codes across thousands of fake accounts.
AgentArmor ties each code to a real login and a real card, so the same card can't claim it twice — in real time,
before the order goes through.

**Dangerous text.** Agents type into notes and fields. AgentArmor cleans hidden commands and classic injection
(SQL, scripts) before they reach your database, your logs, or your AI tools.

## How it works
A normal Shopware plugin. Install it from the Shopware Store, configure it in admin, done — **no proxy, no DNS
changes, no separate server.** It runs inside your store, so no agent data leaves. **Watch-only first:** it logs
what it *would* block so you can see your exposure, then you switch on protection when you're ready.

## FAQ
**Will it slow my checkout?** No — it adds a few milliseconds and never blocks a real order. It runs watch-only
until you decide otherwise.

**Will it block legitimate agents like ChatGPT or Gemini?** No. Known agents are allowlisted; protection targets
untrusted, abusive traffic.

**Does my data leave the store?** No. It runs inside your Shopware install — no cookies, no trackers, no external
calls with your data.
